A few things to know

Before you settle in.

Is Fokasu connected to YPT?

Fokasu is an independent app. It isn't affiliated with YPT (Yeolpumta), and the two apps use separate accounts and study groups.

Can I bring my YPT study history or group?

There is no direct YPT import. You can start a new clan in Fokasu and invite your study friends to join you.

Which computers can run Fokasu?

Current downloads are available for Windows and Fedora. Ubuntu is coming soon. Choose your installer from the Download menu.

How does website blocking work?

Install the Fokasu desktop app and browser extension, choose the sites to block, then create a Focus Block. The Focus Block Guide walks you through it.

Is there a YPT alternative for PC?

Yes. Fokasu is a study and focus app for Windows and Fedora. It is an option for students who like YPT's study tracking and competitive features but want to study from a PC or laptop, with study clans, leaderboards, and distraction blocking.

Does YPT have a desktop version?

YPT (Yeolpumta) is primarily a mobile app. Its official website currently links to the App Store and Google Play, without listing a separate desktop download. Students looking for a similar study experience on Windows or Fedora can try Fokasu.

What is the best YPT alternative for desktop?

The best choice depends on how you study. Fokasu is one option for students looking for a YPT-style study tracker on desktop, particularly if they want website and app blocking alongside study clans, quests, and leaderboards.
