MAKE YOUR STUDY TIME VISIBLE

Track your study hours.
See your progress.

Fokasu automatically records your completed task hours and summarizes where your study time goes with graphs and charts.

01 · STUDY TIME BY SUBJECT

Give every session a subject.

Tag your todos

Assign a tag when creating each todo. Previously created tags are automatically suggested, so you can reuse them across your tasks.

See your daily breakdown

Open Stats to see how your completed task hours for the day are divided across tags.

Review your week by tag

Select a tag in Stats to review your weekly completed task hours for that subject or category.

02 · YOUR DAY, IN VIEW

See where your time went today.

See your subject breakdown

Review your completed study hours by subject in a doughnut chart and see how your time is divided across tags.

Keep your todos organized

See your completed todos in one place. Missed a tag? Click Add Tag to assign one directly from Stats.

Look beyond today

Pick another day or a time span, such as a week or month, to explore your study hours and subject breakdowns.

03 · YOUR PROGRESS OVER TIME

Follow your study trend.

Track your study trend

See how your completed study hours change through the week or month, with a graph that brings your daily progress into view.

See your summary

Review total hours, completed todos, your daily average, best day, and active days for the selected period.

Explore another time span

Pick another week or month to see its study hours, trends, and summary. Look back at how your routine has changed over time.

04 · YOUR YEAR IN ACTIVITY

See your consistency. Build your momentum.

Your year at a glance

See an entire year of study activity in your heatmap. Each square represents a day, with brighter colors showing more study time.

Revisit what you accomplished

Click a day’s square in the app to see the todos you completed and the work you put in.

Keep your momentum going

Watch your activity build over time, celebrate consistent effort, and give yourself another reason to show up tomorrow.

LessMore

05 · GET TO KNOW YOUR STUDY COMMUNITY

Every username has a study story.

Click a username

Click someone’s username in the app to open their profile and see their study summary.

See their recent progress

Review their last three weeks of activity, total study hours, best week, best day, and most recent completed todo.

Build a consistent streak

Your streak reflects your consistency. It resets if you go a day without completing a todo, or have more than three expired todos in the last 15 days.

Make your next study session count.

Available for Windows, Fedora, and Ubuntu.
