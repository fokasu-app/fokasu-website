MAKE FOKASU YOUR OWN

Pro features in Fokasu

A more personal study space, with a fresh look and backgrounds that feel like you.

01 · LIQUID GLASS

A fresh look for your focus.

A little more glass. A whole new feel.

Give Fokasu a fresh look with the Liquid Glass theme. Translucent panels, softly lit buttons, and subtle glass details bring your study space to life.

Your background, your way

Choose your own background images and adjust their opacity. Keep your favorite scene in view, or soften it so your tasks stay in focus.

A fresh view at startup

Turn on random backgrounds to let Fokasu choose an image from your collection whenever the app starts. Give each study session a change of scenery.

02 · PROFILE ICONS & EFFECTS

A profile with a little more personality.

Find an icon that feels like you

Choose from 60+ profile icons, from playful animals to little adventurers. Give your profile a look that stands out in your study circle.

Add a little animation

Pair your icon with an animated effect. Orbiting lights, soft ripples, and twinkling stardust bring a little movement to your profile.

03 · MULTIPLE BLOCKS

Different distractions. Different blocks.

Create your block profiles

Group websites and applications into separate block profiles. Keep a profile for social media, another for entertainment, and choose what each one blocks.

Run up to three blocks

You’re not limited to a single active block. Start up to three blocks at once, so some distractions can stay blocked for longer while you work through a shorter focus session.

End each block separately

Emergency Exit applies to each block independently. End one block early when you need to, while your other blocks keep running.

04 · SCHEDULED BLOCKS

Make focus part of your schedule.

Set it to start automatically

Choose your blocking time ranges and dates. Fokasu applies the block automatically when each scheduled time arrives, so your focus time is already planned.

Create multiple schedules

Plan separate schedules for morning study, afternoon revision, or an evening without distractions. Give each schedule a name and set the times that suit your routine.

Choose a block profile

Link each schedule to a block profile to decide which websites and applications it blocks. Use different profiles for different parts of your day.

05 · MUSIC & AMBIENCE

Find the sound of your focus.

Build your study soundtrack

Download and play lofi tracks or background music right in Fokasu. Choose a mellow beat or a calming sound to settle into your next study session.

Mix it your way

Play lofi and background audio together, with separate controls for each. Enable Sync With Timer to have your music follow your focus timer.

Bring your own music

Import your own music and play your favourite tracks while you study. Keep the sounds that help you focus close at hand.

06 · OFFLINE MODE

Keep studying, even offline.

Work without a connection

Create todos, start your timer, and complete your tasks even when you’re offline. Keep your study session going wherever you are.

Sync when you’re ready

Once you’re back online, sync your offline todos with the server to bring your work up to date.

Choose what to keep

Review your offline todos before syncing. If there are tasks you don’t want to upload, you can delete them instead.

Make room for your next study session.
