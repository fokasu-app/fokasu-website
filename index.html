Frequently asked questions.

What is Fokasu?

Fokasu is a desktop study planner, focus timer, and distraction blocker. You can plan tasks, track focused time, schedule study routines, and join study clans.

Which operating systems does Fokasu support?

Desktop downloads are available for Windows, Fedora, and Ubuntu. macOS support is coming soon.

Can I study with friends?

Yes. Join a study room with your clan to study together with synchronized breaks. You can also chat, complete quests, and compare focused time on the clan leaderboard.

Can Fokasu block websites and desktop apps?

Yes. Set a focus goal and choose the websites and applications you want to block. Completed todo time counts toward your goal; blocking ends when that goal is met. Read the Focus Block Guide.

Can I bypass an active Focus Block?

Focus Blocks are designed to resist everyday workarounds. Closing Fokasu or restarting your computer won’t end an active block; it stays in place until you complete your focus goal. If you need to end a block early, use Emergency Exit in Settings.

Do I need the browser extension?

Website blocking requires the Fokasu browser extension alongside the desktop app. If the extension is missing or disabled during an active Focus Block, Fokasu closes the browser after a grace period, which is 60 seconds by default.
