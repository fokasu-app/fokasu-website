A little more about Fokasu

Frequently asked questions.

Desktop studying, study groups, and fewer distractions.

Is there a YPT alternative for PC?

Yes. Fokasu is a study and focus app for Windows and Fedora. It is an option for students who like YPT's study tracking and competitive features but want to study from a PC or laptop, with study clans, leaderboards, and distraction blocking.

Explore Fokasu as a YPT alternative →

Does YPT have a desktop version?

YPT (Yeolpumta) is primarily a mobile app. Its official website currently links to the App Store and Google Play, without listing a separate desktop download. Students looking for a similar study experience on Windows or Fedora can try Fokasu.

What is the best YPT alternative for desktop?

The best choice depends on how you study. Fokasu is one option for students looking for a YPT-style study tracker on desktop, particularly if they want website and app blocking alongside study clans, quests, and leaderboards.

What is Fokasu?

Fokasu is a desktop study planner, focus timer, and distraction blocker. You can plan tasks, track focused time, schedule study routines, and join study clans.

Which operating systems does Fokasu support?

The current desktop downloads are available for Windows and Fedora. Ubuntu support is coming soon.

Download Fokasu →

Can I study with friends?

Yes. Join a study clan to chat, complete quests, and climb the clan leaderboard together. Fokasu also has study rankings and a community for sharing progress.

Can Fokasu block websites and desktop apps?

Yes. Set a focus goal and choose the websites and applications you want to block. Completed todo time counts toward your goal; blocking ends when that goal is met.

Read the Focus Block Guide →

Do I need the browser extension?

Website blocking requires the Fokasu browser extension alongside the desktop app. If the extension is missing or disabled during an active Focus Block, Fokasu closes the browser after a grace period, which is 60 seconds by default.

Set up the browser extension →

Can I track my study hours?

Yes. Fokasu shows focused time and completed tasks across the week or month so you can see how your study routine is progressing.

Is Fokasu connected to YPT?

No. Fokasu is an independent app and is not affiliated with YPT (Yeolpumta). It is a separate study app, not an official YPT desktop client.
