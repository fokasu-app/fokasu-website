BETTER WITH COMPANY

Study with friends.
See their progress.
Keep each other going.

Send friend requests, create a clan together, complete quests, and level up. Compete alongside your clanmates on the leaderboards.

01FRIENDS

A little company goes a long way.

Live task progress

Follow friends’ ongoing task progress where they’ve enabled sharing. See when your study circle is putting in the work.

Weekly activity

Open a friend’s details to see their shared weekly activity and keep each other motivated.

You control what you share

Choose whether to share your activity. Sharing task progress does not reveal the contents of your tasks.

02YOUR STUDY GROUP

Join a clan. Grow together. Compete as a team.

Create a clan

Pick a name, description, and category. Choose an admission type: Open, Request to join, or Invite-only.

Bring your people together

Invite your friends and members of the community. Follow each other’s shared activity and task progress in real time, and stay connected through clan chat.

Compete on clan leaderboards

Your clan’s cumulative study hours determine its leaderboard ranking. Put in the work together and climb the ranks as a team.

03A SHARED SCHEDULE

Study Together. Take Breaks Together.

Create a study room

Give your room a name, choose the number of sessions, and set the length of each study session and break. Start now or schedule the room for later.

Invite your friends

Tell your friends to join your clan’s study room. The timer stays synchronized for everyone who joins, so you can study on the same schedule.

Take breaks together

Take synchronized breaks and share what you’ve worked on in clan chat. Website blocking is temporarily lifted during the room’s breaks and resumes when the break ends.

04LEADERBOARDS

Put in the hours. Earn your place.

Reach the daily podium

Compete on the daily leaderboards. The top three are featured on every user’s dashboard.

Make the top ten

Rank among the ten users or clans with the most study hours to earn a spot on the Leaderboard page.

Celebrate the weekly winners

Each week, the top three are celebrated with a special animation shared with every user in the application.

YOUR FIRST SESSION TOGETHER

From “let’s study” to getting started.

  1. Get Fokasu

    Install the desktop app on Windows, Fedora, or Ubuntu and sign in.

  2. Find your group

    Join a clan or create one, then invite your study friends.

  3. Settle into a room

    Join a study room, or create one with a study duration, breaks, and session count that suit your group.

Make your next session a shared one.
