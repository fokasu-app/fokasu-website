Extension Privacy

Fokasu privacy policy

Your browsing stays part of your focus session.

Effective August 18, 2026. This policy explains how Fokasu handles information in its desktop application and browser extension.

Browser extension data

Tab URLs

While an active Fokasu focus session is enforcing website blocks, the extension reads tab URLs locally so it can compare them with your configured block and exception patterns. Matching pages are redirected to the extension's blocked page.

The extension does not send tab URLs, browsing history, page contents, titles, cookies, credentials, or form data to Fokasu's remote services. It does not build or retain a browsing-history database.

How data is used and shared

Fokasu uses the information described above only to provide, maintain, and secure its focus-session website-blocking feature. We do not sell it, use it for personalized advertising, or share it with advertising platforms, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. Browser-protection data remains on the user's device.

Choices and settings needed to provide browser protection may be stored locally. They do not contain visited-page history.

Incognito and private browsing

If you enable the extension's incognito or private-window access, Fokasu can enforce the same blocking rules in those windows. The extension does not save or transmit private tab URLs. The same privacy protections described above apply in those windows.

Your choices

On first run, you can decline the local browser connection instead of granting consent. You can later remove the extension, disable its incognito access, or stop using the Fokasu desktop application. Removing the extension removes its locally stored extension data. Settings stored by the desktop app can be removed through the app's own settings or local data removal.

Changes and questions

We will update this page when Fokasu's data practices change. For privacy questions or support, use the support channel at fokasu.app.

In brief

Local blocking. No browsing history sent.

Fokasu reads URLs only to enforce the block rules you chose. The processing stays on your device, and Fokasu does not receive your browsing history through remote services.
