THE FOKASU TOOLKIT

All the features in Fokasu

Todos with deadlines, tags, habits, friends, clans, community, website and app blocking, and much more.

Explore Pro features →

01 · TODOS AND TAGS

Plan your tasks. Keep them organized.

Create a todo

Describe what you want to work on, set a deadline, and choose its estimated focus time.

Organize with tags

Group your todos by subject or category. Existing tags are suggested as you type, making them easy to reuse.

Set your study rhythm

Configure automatic breaks for your todo. A sound notification plays when a todo is completed, a break starts, or a break ends.

02 · TIMERS AND PROGRESS

Keep your focus in view.

A clean task timer

Start your todo with a clean countdown that shows how many minutes remain until it is complete.

Progress bar mode

Pin a slim progress bar to the left edge of your screen. It stays above your apps and follows you across desktops while you work.

Minimized timer mode

Keep a compact timer above your apps. It follows you across desktops so you can check your remaining time without switching windows.

03 · BUILD YOUR ROUTINE

Set your schedule. Keep your habit going.

Todos, created automatically

Choose your habit’s name, cheat days, estimated completion time, schedule, and tags. Fokasu automatically generates todos for your scheduled dates.

Build a habit streak

Each habit has its own streak. Cheat days cover missed scheduled todos; once they’re used up, missing another scheduled todo causes the habit to fail.

Keep going after completion

Finished your habit’s schedule? Extend it to new dates and keep the routine going.

04 · YOUR CLAN, YOUR TEAM

Build a clan. Work toward shared goals.

Make your own study group

Choose your clan’s name, description, and category. Welcome everyone with Open admission, review requests with Request to join, or keep it Invite-only.

Stay connected as you study

Bring friends and community members into your clan. Follow shared activity and live task progress, chat between sessions, and complete quests to level up together.

Climb the ranks together

Your clan’s study hours add up to its place on the clan leaderboard. Contribute to the team and work toward a higher rank together.

05 · CLAN ACTIVITY AND PERKS

Share the effort. Unlock more together.

Complete quests together

Work toward clan quests to help your clan level up. See how much each clanmate has contributed toward your shared goal.

Follow live clan activity

See what your clanmates are currently working on and follow their task progress and status as it updates in real time.

Unlock new clan perks

Level up to unlock more clan logos, shorter waits between chat messages, member promotions, clan announcements, and other perks.

06 · A SHARED SCHEDULE

Study Together. Take Breaks Together.

Create a study room

Give your room a name, choose the number of sessions, and set the length of each study session and break. Start now or schedule the room for later.

Invite your friends

Tell your friends to join your clan’s study room. The timer stays synchronized for everyone who joins, so you can study on the same schedule.

Take breaks together

Take synchronized breaks and share what you’ve worked on in clan chat. Website blocking is temporarily lifted during the room’s breaks and resumes when the break ends.

07 · FRIENDS AND SHARED PROGRESS

Keep your friends close. Keep each other going.

See live task progress

Follow your friends’ ongoing task progress when they’ve enabled sharing. A little shared effort can help you stay motivated.

Check in on their week

Expand a friend’s row to see their weekly activity and how their study time adds up across the days.

Share on your terms

You decide whether to share your activity. Friends can see task progress without seeing the contents of your tasks.

08 · YOUR DAY, IN VIEW

See where your time went today.

See your subject breakdown

Review your completed study hours by subject in a doughnut chart and see how your time is divided across tags.

Keep your todos organized

See your completed todos in one place. Missed a tag? Click Add Tag to assign one directly from Stats.

Look beyond today

Pick another day or a time span, such as a week or month, to explore your study hours and subject breakdowns.

09 · YOUR PROGRESS OVER TIME

Follow your study trend.

Track your study trend

See how your completed study hours change through the week or month, with a graph that brings your daily progress into view.

See your summary

Review total hours, completed todos, your daily average, best day, and active days for the selected period.

Explore another time span

Pick another week or month to see its study hours, trends, and summary. Look back at how your routine has changed over time.

10 · LEADERBOARDS

Put in the hours. Earn your place.

Reach the daily podium

Compete on the daily leaderboards. The top three are featured on every user’s dashboard.

Make the top ten

Rank among the ten users or clans with the most study hours to earn a spot on the Leaderboard page.

Celebrate the weekly winners

Each week, the top three are celebrated with a special animation shared with every user in the application.

11 · YOUR STUDY COMMUNITY

Share your progress. Encourage each other.

Join the conversation

Create posts, leave comments, and connect with other people studying with Fokasu.

Help shape Fokasu

Request features, report bugs, and share feedback with the community to help improve the app.

Keep each other motivated

Share your progress, celebrate milestones, and encourage others as you work toward your goals.

12 · GET TO KNOW YOUR STUDY COMMUNITY

Every username has a study story.

Click a username

Click someone’s username in the app to open their profile and see their study summary.

See their recent progress

Review their last three weeks of activity, total study hours, best week, best day, and most recent completed todo.

Build a consistent streak

Your streak reflects your consistency. It resets if you go a day without completing a todo, or have more than three expired todos in the last 15 days.

13 · FOCUS BLOCKS

Set a focus goal. Make time for it.

Configure your block

Set a focus goal, such as two hours, and choose your break reward. Start the block immediately or schedule it for later, such as 15 minutes from now.

Complete todos to unlock

A two-hour focus goal is met after you complete two hours of todos. Only completed todo time counts toward ending the block.

Leave early when needed

Go to Settings → Emergency Exit to end a block early. During automatic breaks, the block is temporarily lifted. You can also start manual breaks using earned reward minutes from Block Details in the sidebar.

14 · BLOCKLISTS AND EXCEPTIONS

Block distractions. Keep what you need.

Build your blocklist

Add website addresses and select the applications you want to block. Website blocking works with the Fokasu desktop app and browser extension.

Keep useful pages accessible

Use exceptions to keep parts of a blocked website available. The wildcard * matches any text: adding reddit.com/r/* to your blocklist blocks every subreddit.

Choose what to hide on YouTube

Hide distractions such as recommendations, the home feed, comments, thumbnails, and Shorts while keeping access to the videos you need.

15 · D-DAY COUNTDOWN

Keep your next big date in sight.

Choose your header display

Show the online user count or switch to D-Day to keep a countdown visible in the app header.

Pick a date to count down to

Select a date within the next three years. Fokasu shows how many days remain, right beside your workspace.

Keep important dates in view

Count down to your next exam, deadline, or milestone and keep the date you’re working toward in sight.

16 · YOUR YEAR IN ACTIVITY

See your consistency. Build your momentum.

Your year at a glance

See an entire year of study activity in your heatmap. Each square represents a day, with brighter colors showing more study time.

Revisit what you accomplished

Click a day’s square in the app to see the todos you completed and the work you put in.

Keep your momentum going

Watch your activity build over time, celebrate consistent effort, and give yourself another reason to show up tomorrow.
