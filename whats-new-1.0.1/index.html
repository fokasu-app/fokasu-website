01 · Todos with breaks

Focus deeply. Break automatically.

For example, create a 4-hour todo with Automatic Breaks enabled (default is a 10-minute break after every hour). After each 1-hour, Fokasu automatically starts a 10-minute break.

Skip a break Keep working when you do not need the scheduled break.
Shift a break Move the break and take it after your next session instead.
Temporary access During a break, blocked websites and applications are temporarily unlocked.

02 · Scheduled block

Start your block when you’re ready.

You can now begin your block after a selected number of minutes. If you tend to procrastinate, set when you want your block to begin and let Fokasu handle the rest.
