Is Fokasu a fit for you?

Fokasu suits students who want to plan tasks, study alongside others, and block distractions on their computer. Downloads are available for Windows, Fedora, and Ubuntu; macOS support is coming soon.

If you need a phone-first workflow or a record of every elapsed minute, consider those needs before choosing. Fokasu’s completed-task statistics reflect the durations you set for tasks.

Frequently asked questions.

How do I start a study session?

Create a todo, give it an estimated focus time, and start its timer from the dashboard. You can also set a deadline and configure automatic breaks for the task.

Can I choose when to take breaks?

Yes. When creating a todo, use the automatic break settings to choose a study interval and break duration that suit your routine.

What happens to blocking during an automatic break?

During an approved automatic break, Fokasu temporarily lifts website and application blocking. If your Focus Block is still active, blocking resumes when the break ends. Read the Focus Block Guide.

Can my friends and I study at the same time?

Yes. Join a clan and use its study rooms to focus together on a shared schedule, including synchronized breaks. You can also chat with your clan and work toward clan quests.

Can I see my timer while using another app?

Yes. Switch to progress bar mode to keep a compact progress bar above your other apps. You can keep studying in your notes or browser while checking your session progress.

Can I track how much time I spend on each subject?

Add subject tags to your todos, then filter Stats by tag. Totals use the focus duration set for completed tasks, grouped by completion date, rather than every elapsed timer minute. See how study time tracking works.
