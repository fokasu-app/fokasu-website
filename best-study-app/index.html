Fokasu is a desktop study app that brings your tasks, study friends, and distraction blocking together.

01

Study together

Study together in clan rooms with synced break times, see what your clanmates are working on, and compare focused time on the leaderboards. Complete quests and level up your clan together.

Fokasu
02

Keep breaks from taking over

A five-minute break can turn into half an hour of scrolling. Plan your breaks in a todo and start a Focus Block. Fokasu lifts blocking during your break, then automatically blocks distracting websites and apps again when break time is over.

03

Keep progress in view

Switch to progress bar mode and pin your progress above the apps you're using. Keep your notes, browser, or textbook in view while the bar grows at the edge of your screen.
