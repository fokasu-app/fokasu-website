Refund & Cancellation Policy

Updated November 17, 2026. This policy applies to Fokasu Pro subscriptions purchased through Paddle, whether you choose monthly or yearly billing.

14-day first-purchase guarantee

If Pro is not right for you, request a full refund within 14 calendar days of your first paid Pro purchase. This guarantee applies to both monthly and yearly plans, even if you have used Pro during that time. It is offered by Fokasu and does not restart with each renewal.

How to request a refund

Email fokasu.support@gmail.com with the subject “Fokasu Pro refund request.” Include your purchase email address and Paddle transaction or order reference so we can find the payment. Do not send your password or full payment-card details.

You can also request a refund through Paddle support or the support link in your receipt. We will arrange eligible refunds through Paddle, which processes the payment. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible; the time before funds appear depends on Paddle and your payment provider.

Canceling your subscription

You can cancel at any time using the “Manage subscription” link in your Paddle confirmation email, or request cancellation through Paddle support. Cancel before your next renewal to prevent the next charge.

Cancellation stops future renewals. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. Cancellation alone does not refund the current billing period, and uninstalling Fokasu does not cancel your subscription.

Renewals and other refund requests

Outside the first-purchase guarantee, we do not normally refund renewal charges or unused time in a billing period. If you were charged by mistake, see a duplicate charge, or have a technical problem with Pro, contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

This does not limit any refund or withdrawal rights under applicable law or Paddle’s Refund Policy, including Paddle’s ability to issue discretionary refunds. Mandatory consumer rights always apply.

After a refund

When a Pro purchase is fully refunded, the Pro access associated with that purchase ends. You can continue using Fokasu’s free features. If you also want to stop future payments, cancel your subscription or ask support to confirm that it has been canceled.

Questions

Fokasu is operated by an independent individual under the Fokasu brand. For help with this policy, contact fokasu.support@gmail.com. You can also read our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

In brief

A little room to decide.

Request a full refund within 14 days of your first Pro payment. Cancel to stop future renewals. Paddle processes refunds, and mandatory consumer rights remain protected.
